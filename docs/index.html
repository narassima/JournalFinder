How it works

  1. Scope mapping — your text is matched to 4,500 research topics, then to journals that actually publish on those topics (OpenAlex publication profiles).
  2. Quality signals — SJR, quartile, H-index, citedness and the ABDC rating are added for each journal.
  3. Justified ranking — each recommendation explains the scope fit, metrics, ranking status and any cautions.
